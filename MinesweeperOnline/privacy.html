Privacy policy

What Minesweeper Online collects, why, who else handles it and how long it is kept. It covers the Android app, the Windows app and the web version.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

  • Your account is kept on our server: the name you choose, an optional country, your devices, and your games, scores and coins.
  • Other players see your name and flag, and your scores, ratings, statistics, achievements and replays.
  • Ads appear only in the Android phone app, and only when you choose to watch one. Google provides them.
  • There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in the game. We don't sell your data.
  • You can have your account deleted at any time: see Delete your account.

Your account

Every installation of the game gets an account on our server the first time it connects. You don't give us an email address, a phone number or your real name.

  • Name. Any name you like. It is shown to other players. We keep the names an account has used before, to deal with abuse.
  • Country. Optional, and chosen by you. It is shown as a flag next to your name. “Global” shows no flag.
  • Friend code. A number that identifies your account, so friends can find you.
  • Sign-in keys. Your device keeps the keys that sign it in to your account. Our server keeps what it needs to check them.
  • Transfer phrase. To add another device, you can create a one-time phrase. It works once and expires after ten minutes.

Your devices

  • Install code. The Android and Windows apps send a code computed from your device's system identifier (on Android, its Android ID) together with your account. The code can't be turned back into that identifier, and it is different for every account. We use it to tell your devices apart and to notice when the game's data was copied to another device. The web version sends none.
  • Device name. While your account is used on more than one device, each device sends its name, so the others can show which is which: the name set in Android's settings (or the phone's model), the computer's name on Windows, or the browser and system on the web, such as “Chrome (Windows)”. It is kept while the account has more than one device.
  • Use. When each device was added and last connected, which platform and version of the game it runs, and how long its sessions last.

Your games

The server keeps the results of your online games and Solo runs, so they follow your account to every device: times, scores, ratings, statistics, achievements, Community results, the levels you have cleared and the days you played.

A run that makes a leaderboard keeps a replay: the board and your moves. Leaderboards, player profiles and replays can be seen by every player.

Coins and items

The server keeps your coin balance and your items, a record of what you earned and spent and when, your daily bonus streak, and the rewarded ads you watched (when, and the transaction code Google sends with the reward). Coins can't be bought. A donation never gives coins or items.

Chat

Chat is optional. A message you send goes to the players in your game. The server passes it on and doesn't save it. To deal with abuse, we can record one game's chat while that game runs. That record is kept in the server's log.

Reports and moderation

Players can report each other, and the server reports some cheating by itself. A report keeps the reported account, its name, its IP address and platform, the time and the reason. Accounts that break the rules can be banned.

Connections and logs

Our server records each connection: the time, the IP address and port, the game's version and how signing in went. Its log names the accounts it serves. We use these records to keep the game running and to stop abuse and cheating.

Connections reach our server through ngrok, a relay service, which sees the IP address. Every connection is encrypted: from the Android and Windows apps all the way to our server, so ngrok passes it on unread, and from the web version to ngrok, which passes it on to us.

Ads (Android phone app only)

The Android phone app shows an ad only when you press a button to watch one, for coins or for a revive. There are no banners and no ads between games. The Windows app, the web version and Android TV show no ads.

The ads come from Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your IP address, your interactions with ads (such as taps and video views), diagnostic information, and your device's advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google sends this data encrypted. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the first time you press an ad button, Google's consent form asks how your data may be used for ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

When you finish an ad, Google tells our server, and the server adds your reward. We send Google a one-time code with each ad, so the reward reaches the right account. Google doesn't tell us who you are.

Google Play Games (Android)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game asks Google to remember which game account belongs to your Play Games profile, so your account comes back on a new device. We keep only a random code for that link, and Google keeps the link. We don't receive your Google account's name, email address or Play Games ID. You can stop it in Settings → Account with Unlink.

On your device

The game keeps your settings, progress, saved games and sign-in keys on your device: in the app's own storage on Android and Windows, and in your browser's storage on the web. Android may include them in its backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps; you control that in Android's settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself. No push-notification service is used.

Who else handles your data

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for their own purposes. These services handle some of it for us:

  • Google: Google Play (the store), Google Play Games, AdMob (ads) and Android's backup.
  • ngrok: relays connections to our server.
  • GitHub: hosts this website, the web version of the game and the list of server addresses the game reads. Like any website, it sees the IP addresses of visitors.
  • PayPal: handles donations. We see what PayPal shows the recipient of a donation, such as the donor's name, email address and the amount.

These companies may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards. We disclose data to others only when the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • After deletion, leaderboard times, ratings, statistics, achievements, Community results and replays stay under a name like “Deleted 7”, without your name, country or friend code.
  • Reports, bans, past names, server logs and connection records, including IP addresses, as long as needed to prevent abuse and cheating.
  • Backups of the server's data are deleted after a limited time.
  • Anything on your device, until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your name or country in the game at any time.
  • Unlink Google Play Games in Settings → Account.
  • Change your ad choices in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices, or don't watch ads at all: nothing in the game needs them.
  • Ask for your account to be deleted: Delete your account.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to restrict how we use it, or to object to it, and you can complain to your data protection authority. We use your data to give you the game you asked for (your account, games, leaderboards and coins), in our legitimate interest in keeping the game fair and safe (logs, reports and moderation), and, for personalised ads, with the consent you give in Google's form.

Children

Minesweeper Online is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes are announced in the game.
