📱 Account in use elsewhere

Your account is currently being used on another device. You can take over the session here — this will disconnect the other device.

Take over the session on this device? The other device will be disconnected immediately and any game in progress there will be forfeited.

Leave this account on this device? This device will be removed from the account and a fresh local account will be created here. Your other devices keep the account.

Another copy of this device uses the same sign-in. Leaving signs it out too.

This will permanently wipe all of your achievements, statistics and completed levels on this device. Your other devices keep their data. This cannot be undone.

This account is open on a copy of this device

Another copy of this device signed in with the same details, so this one was signed out. That usually means the game's data was copied: a cloned disk, a copied browser profile, a restored PC image. Only one copy can be online at a time.
