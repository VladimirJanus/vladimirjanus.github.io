Delete your account

Send us an email and we will delete your Minesweeper Online account. The button opens an email with what we need already filled in.

@ Email a deletion request vladjsoftware+delete@gmail.com

Include your in-game name and, if you still have the game, the friend code from Settings → Account. We may ask you to prove the account is yours, for example by changing your in-game name to a code we send you.

What is deleted

Your devices, your sign-in keys and the link to Google Play Games. Your name, country and friend code are taken off the account. Nobody can sign in to it again, and a new install starts a new account. We act on requests within the time the law allows.

What stays, without your name

Leaderboard times, ratings, statistics, achievements, Community results and replays stay under a name like “Deleted 7”. This keeps the leaderboards complete and lets us check them for cheating.

What is kept to prevent abuse

Reports, bans, past names, server logs and connection records, including IP addresses, are kept as long as needed to prevent abuse and cheating. Backups are deleted after a limited time. Anything saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the game.

More about what the game collects is in the privacy policy.
