Privacy Policy

This policy describes what data BetterTimetable collects, why, and how it is handled.

BetterTimetable is an independent app designed for FERI schedules. It is not an official FERI app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FERI. Timetable data comes from the public Wise Timetable exports.

BetterTimetable collects nothing.
