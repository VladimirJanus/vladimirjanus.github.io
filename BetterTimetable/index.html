BetterTimetable

An alternative to Wise Timetable. Choose your course groups, find a plan that fits, and keep your schedule together on Windows and Android.

Designed for University of Maribor timetables, with FERI selected by default. An independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the university, its faculties, or Wise Timetable.
