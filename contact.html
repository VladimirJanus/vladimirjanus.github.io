Before you write

What helps most in a bug report

Include the app name, version, platform and what happened. For BetterTimetable, include your faculty, programme, date and view; a screenshot helps with layout problems. For Minesweeper Online, include the game mode and your in-game username. Please leave personal events and other private information out of screenshots or shared backups.
